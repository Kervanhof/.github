# Kervanhof

**From Turkish workshops to the world's markets.**
*Türk ustasının ürünü, dünyanın pazarında. · Aus türkischen Werkstätten auf die Märkte der Welt.*

Kervanhof helps Turkish makers and brands sell the products they already make to customers abroad, starting with Germany.

Trade caravans (*kervan*) once rested in the courtyard (*Hof*) of a caravanserai before carrying goods to distant markets. Kervanhof is that courtyard for today's makers.

## What we do

- **Product pages in the buyer's language.** German first: listings, product stories and label texts.
- **Compliance, coordinated.** Together with specialist partners, we make sure makers know what the EU asks for (product safety, packaging registration, labelling) and who takes care of it.
- **An honest check first.** Before anything else, we work out whether a product can make money in the target market, and we say "not yet" when it can't.
- **Customer service after the sale.** Messages and returns from foreign customers, answered in their language.

## How it works

1. **Check:** we look at three of your products and tell you honestly whether they can sell abroad.
2. **Start:** we prepare your products for one sales channel, such as a German version of your online store, or Etsy.
3. **Grow:** we handle customer messages and keep your listings up to date as orders come in.

## Our principles

- The product is yours, and so is the brand.
- We never promise guaranteed sales.
- Everyone who works with us is paid fairly for their time.
- We are open about who does the work and where.

## Where we are

Based in Samsun, Türkiye. Our first market is Germany, and more countries will follow. We are launching in 2026 with a small group of makers and growing from real results.
